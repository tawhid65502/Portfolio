A Bit About Me

Geography, GIS & Environmental Research

I hold both a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Geography and Environment from Jagannath University, with CGPAs of 3.75 and 3.78 respectively. My academic background has built a strong foundation in geographical analysis, environmental studies and spatial thinking.

My academic and research interests include GIS and Remote Sensing, cyclone damage assessment, environmental analysis, land-use studies, water quality and the application of geospatial techniques to better understand environmental and spatial challenges.

Professionally, I work as an Assistant GIS Analyst at Sheltech Consultants, where I contribute to geospatial data collection, mapping, land-use analysis, GIS database management and urban-planning initiatives. I have also worked as a GIS Expert and Volunteer with GROUPMAPPERS on GIS and community projects.
